# DIMs: Dumb Intelligent Mutants — Player Guide

Welcome to SCUM's weirdest feature. The DIMs are zombies who got too smart for their own good. They can't fight, but they sure can talk — and they remember you.

## What Are DIMs?

DIMs (Dumb Intelligent Mutants) are special NPCs scattered across the island. They look like zombies and move like zombies, but they've somehow developed actual personalities. Nobody knows how it happened, and honestly, nobody's asking too many questions.

**Key facts:**
- They don't attack players
- They don't take damage
- They won't help you fight
- But they will absolutely talk your ear off about weapons, medicine, car parts, and their various existential crises

Think of them as walking gossip columnists with encyclopedic knowledge of the island.

---

## How to Talk to a DIM

### Step 1: Get Close
DIMs can only hear Local chat — the kind of message that broadcasts to players near you on the map. Make sure you're within **50 meters** of the DIM.

### Step 2: Switch to Local Chat
Press **Enter** to open the chat box. Make sure you're on the **Local** channel (not Global). You'll see a green indicator if Local is active.

### Step 3: Say Something
Type their name or one of their keywords:

```
/Local Ziggy
/Local Hey Ziggy!
/Local Shop?
```

DIMs don't need perfect grammar. Use their name, a keyword, or both.

### Step 4: They'll Respond
If they heard you and aren't busy, they'll respond in Local chat. Everyone nearby will see the conversation.

## Meet the DIMs

### Ziggy — The Arms Dealer
**Appears:** Military camps, bunkers, hidden weapon caches  
**Personality:** Paranoid, calculating, warms up if you're friendly  
**Specialties:** Weapons, ammo, combat gear

**Keywords:** `ziggy` `trader` `shop` `buy` `sell` `gun` `ammo` `weapon`

Ziggy was a soldier once. He's seen things, survived things, and now he hoards information about every weapon stash on the island. He's suspicious of strangers but if you prove yourself, he'll point you toward guns, ammo, and gear that most players never find. 

*Example conversation:*
```
You: Ziggy, where can I find a rifle?
Ziggy: Depends on what you can trade for it. Find me again if you're serious.
```

**Appears:** Medical facilities, research labs, hospitals  
**Personality:** Exhausted, dark humor, surprisingly competent  
**Specialties:** Medicine, healing items, biochemistry

**Keywords:** `vera` `doc` `doctor` `heal` `medicine` `drug` `cure` `blood` `plasma`

She's tired, sarcastic, and absolutely knows where every medical supply on the island is hidden. Need antibiotics? She knows. Blood plasma? She has a map.

*Example conversation:*
```
You: Doc, how do I stop an infection?
Vera: Antibiotics. Usually in the medical tent west of the airfield. If you make it that far without bleeding out.
```

### Rust — The Mechanic
**Appears:** Garages, repair shops, vehicle graveyards  
**Personality:** Paranoid, talks to inanimate objects, dedicated

**Keywords:** `rust` `scavenger` `junk` `parts` `mechanic` `car` `engine` `wheel` `fuel` `vehicle`

## How Conversations Work

### The 90-Second Window
Once you get a DIM's attention, they'll keep talking to you for **90 seconds** without requiring their name again. During this window, you can ask follow-up questions or keep the conversation going. After 90 seconds of silence, they'll stop listening.

```
You: Hey Ziggy!                      ← DIM attention activated
Ziggy: What do you want?
You: Where are the guns?              ← Still in 90-second window
Ziggy: East sector, buried cache.
You: Any ammo nearby?                 ← Still in window
Ziggy: Yeah, same place.
[90 seconds pass with no messages]
You: What about grenades?             ← Window closed, Ziggy won't hear you
→ Use their name again to restart
```

### What They Won't Tell You
DIMs won't:
- Give you exact GPS coordinates (they're not that smart)
- Mark locations on your map
- Fight alongside you
- Trade items directly

They will:
- Give vague-to-decent directions
- Point you toward zones and landmarks
- Describe what you'll find
- Remember your face and personality

## Reputation System

Every time you talk to a DIM, your **reputation** with them changes. Be nice, and they'll help you. Be rude, and they'll give you the cold shoulder.

### Checking Your Rep
Type this in Local chat:
```
/Local !rep ziggy
```

The DIM will tell you where you stand.

### Reputation Tiers

| Tier | Score | What Happens |
|------|-------|---|
| **Enemy** | 0–25 | They ignore or mock you. Minimal help. |
| **Neutral** | 26–50 | They'll talk to you but don't trust you. Generic responses. |
| **Acquaintance** | 51–75 | Friendly. They'll give you better hints. |
| **Friend** | 76–100 | You're in. They'll tell you exactly where the good loot is. |

### How to Raise Reputation
- **Be polite** — Start with greetings
- **Ask about their specialty** — Ziggy loves talking about weapons, Doc Vera about medicine
- **Show interest** — Listen to what they say, ask follow-up questions
- **Revisit them** — Long-term relationships matter

### How to Lower Reputation
- **Be hostile** — Threats, insults, aggression
- **Ignore them** — Never come back
- **Ask them for help, then disappear** — Say you'll follow their directions, never do it

## Pro Tips

### 1. Start With a Greeting
First impressions matter. Don't just bark orders.
```
Good: "Hey Ziggy, how's it going?"
Bad: "Ziggy, WHERE ARE WEAPONS??"
```

### 2. Play to Their Strengths
Each DIM has an expertise. Ask about it.
```
Ziggy → weapons, ammo, combat gear
Doc Vera → medicine, healing, supplies
Rust → vehicles, parts, transportation
```

### 3. Your Reputation Persists
If you build a good relationship with a DIM, they'll remember you next session. Invest early, profit later.

### 4. Only Local Chat Works
DIMs cannot hear Global chat. You must use Local. Global will broadcast your message to the whole server, but the DIM will ignore it.

```
Good: /Local Ziggy, where are weapons?
Bad: /Global Ziggy, where are weapons?
```

### 5. No Client Mods Needed
DIMs are 100% server-side. You don't need to install anything. Just run the game normally.

### 6. They're Spread Out
DIMs hang around the locations they know best. Want to meet Ziggy? Head to military camps. Looking for Doc Vera? Try hospitals. Need Rust? Visit a garage.

### 7. Bring Friends
Other players can see and hear the conversation when you talk to a DIM. It's public chat, so strategies and loot hints get shared. That's half the fun.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I trade items with DIMs?**  
A: No. They just give advice.

**Q: What if a DIM is hostile to me?**  
A: They'll still talk, but they'll be cagey and unhelpful. Build up reputation to improve the relationship.

**Q: Can I find DIMs on the map?**  
A: No markers on your compass. You have to explore. That's part of the challenge.

**Q: Do DIMs respawn if I kill them?**  
A: You can't kill them. They're unkillable NPCs. Invulnerable, but non-aggressive.

**Q: What if the server resets?**  
A: Reputation might reset depending on server settings. Check with your server admin.

**Q: Can I talk to a DIM if they're far away?**  
A: No. You need to be within **50 meters** and use **Local chat**. If they're too far, you'll have to walk closer.

**Q: Do DIMs say anything without being talked to?**  
A: Rarely. They're mostly quiet until someone addresses them.
